# Meeting summaries in All Recordings

## Conversation Evidence

> owner (paraphrased, 2026-11-16): in the All Recordings window, each recording should show a short summary of what the meeting was about, so the overview already tells which entry is which.
> owner (paraphrased, 2026-11-16): the summary comes from the generated meeting notes or the transcript, and the owner can edit it.
> owner (paraphrased, 2026-11-16): accepted both mockup boards as drawn: the list with a grey two-line summary under each title and the search hint "Search titles and summaries", and the Edit Summary sheet.

## Goal & Context

<!-- Source: 0% user / 55% [paraphrase] / 45% [inferred] -->

All Recordings lists each meeting with its title, app, date and length. Many titles are generic: the meeting app's name, an imported file's name, "New Recording 3" or "Recovered Recording (…)". To find out which row is which meeting, the user opens the notes one by one. [paraphrase]

After this spec, every meeting whose notes the app saves from this version on carries a short summary of what it was about, shown in grey under its title in All Recordings. When the notes have a summary section, the app takes the summary from it. When they have none, the app asks the notes provider for a short summary of the finished notes, in the background after the notes are saved. The user can change the summary or write one for any finished meeting, and new notes never replace a summary the user wrote. Meetings from before this version get none unless the user types one. The search finds words in summaries too. [paraphrase]

Who it is for: everyone who records or imports meetings with the app. Why now: the owner asked for it on 2026-11-16. It builds on the rows and the row menu that #171 (Transcribe Again, Identify Speakers Again) changes, and says what happens to the summary when a meeting is processed again. [inferred]

Upstream check (2026-11-16): the original project has no issue or pull request about a summary per recording or about editing one. Related only: its open issue 873 proposes a separate "Meetings" window that shows a meeting's notes and transcript side by side (no pull request yet); its open PR 990 adds named notes templates, which is why the summary rule below does not depend on the built-in template's layout; its open PR 979 passes the meeting title to the notes prompt and does not change the title the list shows. In the fork, #126 (the search finds no participants, since few meetings have any) and #201 (All Recordings must not wait on a volume that stops answering) bear on R6 and on the rule that the window reads no notes file for a summary.

## Architecture & Data Models

- **The summary of a meeting** is one plain-text paragraph of at most 600 characters. The app keeps up to two per meeting: the **notes summary**, which the app got from the notes it last saved for the meeting, and the **typed summary**, which the user wrote. The row, its tooltip, the sheet and the search use the typed summary when there is one, else the notes summary (A15). A typed summary stays until the user changes or empties it. [inferred]
- **Where it is kept:** its own file in the app's data folder, beside the finished-job list, keyed by the meeting's job id: per meeting each of the two texts with when it was written. Neither the meeting record (gh-97, `MeetingFileRecord`) nor the finished-job list (`TerminalJobRecord`) holds it: meetings from before the record have no record, and a build that does not know a field drops it when it rewrites either file, which would lose a typed summary (A8). The file is written atomically and readable only by the user, as the record file is, and two running builds of the fork take turns through a lock beside it, as they do for the record file (`MeetingFileStore`). [inferred]
- **The summary section rule** is one pure function of the saved notes' text (A13). It looks for the first heading, at any level, whose text without Markdown marks, a leading number ("1.") and a trailing colon, compared ignoring case and diacritics, is or begins with one of the summary names: "Summary" and the word for it in each notes language the app offers (`AppSettings.protocolLanguages`), for example "Zusammenfassung", "Résumé", "Resumen", "Samenvatting". The section runs to the next heading and never past the separator before an appended full transcript (`ProtocolGenerator.fullTranscriptSeparator`). From it the rule drops tables, HTML comments, horizontal rules, label lines such as `**Date:** …` and paragraphs wholly in square brackets (a model that copied the template's placeholder), removes list and block-quote markers and Markdown marks (emphasis, inline code, link targets), joins what is left with single spaces and cuts it to 600 characters at a word end, adding "…". An empty result counts as no summary section. In notes from the built-in template this is the Summary section. [inferred]
- **The summary call** (D2, A14): when the saved notes have no summary section, the app asks the notes provider for a summary of them, after the notes are saved and in the background. It takes a generator from the same factory the notes came from (`PipelineQueue.protocolGeneratorFactory`, `ProtocolRegenerator`'s `generatorFactory`, both built by `PipelineController.makeProtocolGenerator()`), asked once right after the save, so the call uses the provider set in Settings at that moment; with provider None the factory gives none and there is no call. The request is the generator's own `generate` with the notes as its text (without an appended full transcript, cut to the first 32,000 characters at a line end), the meeting's title, `diarized: false`, no meeting time, no background, and in place of the notes template the fixed instruction `MeetingSummary.instruction`: "Write a summary of the following meeting notes in {LANGUAGE}: one paragraph of plain text, at most three sentences and at most 600 characters, saying what the meeting was about and what was decided. Return only the summary, with no heading, list, Markdown or introduction." followed by a blank line and "Meeting notes:". The answer goes through the same clean-up as a section's text; an empty result stores nothing. Calls run one at a time, apart from the pipeline's queue: the job reaches Done and the queue goes on before the call starts. A local model's deferral for a running recording is waited out as for the notes. A call still running after 180 seconds is cancelled and counts as failed. [paraphrase]
- **When a summary is taken:** wherever the app saves a meeting's current notes, from the text it saved, never by reading the file back: the notes of a new meeting, Retry Meeting Notes and Generate Meeting Notes from Transcript… (all three through `PipelineQueue.saveGeneratedNotes` in `PipelineQueue+Stages.swift`), a regeneration that replaces the main notes of a meeting that has a job (`ProtocolRegenerator`'s `regenerated` hook in `ProtocolRegeneration.swift`, from the row's Regenerate Notes… with "Replace the current notes" or from "Regenerate from File…" on a file of a listed meeting, A19), and the success step of Transcribe Again and Identify Speakers Again (#171), not the re-run's own save under names of its own. A found summary section, or a call's answer, becomes the meeting's notes summary; a typed summary stays as it is. A save that gets no summary (provider None, a failed or empty call) leaves the notes summary as it was (A18). An additional report (gh-190) and "Regenerate from File…" on a file of no listed meeting change no summary. [inferred]
- **Meetings from before this version** (D3): they have no summary until the user types one or the app saves new notes for them (A16). The app reads no notes file to make a summary, at launch, when the window opens or from the sheet, and offers no "Summarize" item. [paraphrase]
- **The row** (`TranscriptionsView`, `TranscriptionsView+RowParts.swift`) reads the summary from the store in memory, never from a file while it is drawn. The row's list entry (`TranscriptionEntry`) carries the summary it shows, so the search reads it too (`TranscriptionList.matching`). [inferred]
- **"Edit Summary…"** is one more item of the pure row decision (`RecordingRowActions.menuItems`), so the "…" menu and the right-click menu list it together. It opens a sheet over All Recordings, like the regenerate sheet (`RegenerateSheetState`). The sheet's state lives in one `@Observable` object, so a ViewInspector test can read back what a control wrote (gui-testing doc, rung 2). [inferred]
- **The accepted row** (D6, D10): the summary is one more line directly under the title line (the title and its Keep badge), above the "app · date · length" line, in the secondary text colour, smaller than the title and larger than the lines below it. It shows at most two lines, cut at the end with "…", and hovering the line shows the whole summary as a tooltip. A row without a summary looks as today. The line shows in every state of the row, so a meeting being transcribed again keeps it. [paraphrase]
- **The accepted Edit Summary sheet** (D11), top to bottom: the heading "Edit Summary"; below it in secondary text the meeting's title, date and length as the row shows them, joined with " · " (for example "Weekly team sync · 16 Nov 2026 at 09:00 · 48:12"); a multi-line text field about five lines high with the summary the row shows (empty when it has none), focused when the sheet opens; one line in secondary text under the field (below); and a bottom row with "Use Summary from Notes" at the left and Cancel and Save at the right, Save the default button. The line under the field reads "Taken from the meeting notes. Once you change it, new notes for this meeting no longer replace it." while the meeting shows its notes summary, and "You wrote this summary. New notes for this meeting do not replace it." while it shows a typed one. A meeting with no summary shows no line, and while the field holds more than 600 characters the line reads "At most 600 characters." (A17). [paraphrase]

## API Contracts

- No new endpoint. The summary is not part of `JobStatusDTO`, `GET /v1/jobs/<id>` or any other `/v1` response, and All Recordings stays off the `/ui/tree` and `/ui/press` window allowlist, since it shows meeting content. [inferred]
- The summary call uses the existing generator interface (`ProtocolGenerating.generate`) and the provider settings as they are; no new setting. [inferred]
- The summary file is new and only this fork reads it. The meeting record file and the finished-job list keep their formats unchanged, so a build without this spec reads them as before. [inferred]

## Edge Cases & Constraints

- **Notes in another language:** the rule knows the summary heading of every notes language the app offers, so German notes ("## Zusammenfassung") work as English ones do. A summary heading the rule does not know counts as none, and the call makes the summary instead. [inferred]
- **Custom templates (gh-2, gh-207):** notes with a summary section, whatever its place, give that section; notes without one get the call. A summary section that is a list gives its items joined as one paragraph. [paraphrase]
- **No notes:** protocol provider None, failed notes, notes generation off for a re-run (#171 A12) and meetings whose notes were never saved leave the summary as it was; a row without one shows no summary line. A Retry Meeting Notes that succeeds fills it. [inferred]
- **Notion export that replaces the local Markdown:** the summary is taken, or the call made, from the generated text, which the app has in memory before the local copy is removed. [inferred]
- **Transcribe Again and Identify Speakers Again (#171):** while the re-run waits or runs, the row keeps showing the meeting's current summary, since its notes stay current until the re-run succeeds. At success the new notes give the notes summary by the rules above; a typed one stays. A re-run that fails, is cancelled or stays unnamed leaves the summary as it was. Earlier versions (`.until-<stamp>`) and `.previous-<stamp>` copies get no summary of their own. [inferred]
- **During a call:** the row shows the meeting's earlier summary, or none for a new meeting, until the answer arrives. A newer notes save for the same meeting makes an older call's answer void; a meeting moved to the Trash during its call stores nothing; a summary typed during the call stays shown, and the answer becomes the notes summary. A quit during the call stores nothing, and the call is not made again at the next launch. [inferred]
- **Notes edited outside the app** do not change the summary. "Use Summary from Notes" offers the notes summary of the notes the app last saved, not of the edited file (A15). [inferred]
- **No notes file is read for a summary:** a notes file that is gone, unreadable or on a volume that is not connected or does not answer changes nothing, and the window never waits on one (#201). [inferred]
- **Record-only mode:** recordings made in it have no row, so nothing changes for them; rows from before stay editable. [inferred]
- **Privacy:** a summary is meeting content, and so are the notes the call sends. Neither appears in a log line, a notification, a job warning, the automation API or a test fixture; tests use made-up text. The call's log lines name the job's short id, the provider and the outcome only. [inferred]
- **Wording:** on screen the app says "notes" (gh-97 A11) and "summary". [inferred]

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1:** The app keeps each meeting's notes summary and typed summary in their own file keyed by the job id, written atomically and readable only by the user, and loads it at launch. Errors: a file that cannot be read or decoded is moved aside under an `.unreadable` name before anything new is written, and the app starts with no summaries; a failed write is logged without content and never fails a job or an action; while the other running build holds the lock longer than the wait bound, the write fails and is logged, as for the record file. [inferred]
- **R2:** Each time the app saves a meeting's current notes (a new meeting, Retry Meeting Notes, Generate Meeting Notes from Transcript…, a regeneration that replaces the main notes of a meeting with a job, the success step of Transcribe Again and Identify Speakers Again), it applies the summary section rule to the saved text; a found section becomes the notes summary, and notes without one start the summary call of R5. A typed summary stays unchanged throughout. Errors: a save that gets no summary leaves the notes summary as it was; an additional report, "Regenerate from File…" on a file of no listed meeting, failed notes and a re-run that fails, is cancelled or stays unnamed change no summary and make no call; with the Notion export replacing the local Markdown the summary is still taken or the call still made. [paraphrase]
- **R3:** A meeting with a summary shows it in its All Recordings row as the accepted row describes (Architecture): directly under the title, in the secondary text colour, at most two lines cut with "…", the whole text as the tooltip of that line. Errors: a row without a summary looks as today; a meeting that is being transcribed again keeps showing its summary until the re-run succeeds; drawing a row reads no file. [paraphrase]
- **R4:** Every finished row offers "Edit Summary…" in its "…" menu and its right-click menu, directly after "Show in Finder" (in that place when the row lists no Show in Finder), also when it has no notes or no record. It opens the accepted Edit Summary sheet (Architecture). Save stores the text, trimmed and with line breaks joined by single spaces, as the typed summary, and the row shows it at once; a text equal to the notes summary is stored as the notes summary instead, with no typed one; Cancel and Escape change nothing. "Use Summary from Notes" puts the meeting's notes summary into the field, reads no file and makes no call. Errors: saving an empty text removes both summaries, so the meeting's next notes fill it again; Save is disabled while the text is longer than 600 characters; "Use Summary from Notes" is disabled for a meeting without a notes summary; a Save for a meeting moved to the Trash while the sheet was open stores nothing; not offered for a waiting, running, naming or failed row, nor in the menu of a multi-row selection. [paraphrase]
- **R5:** When saved notes have no summary section and the factory gives a generator, the app makes the summary call as Architecture describes it: after the save, in the background, one call at a time, with the notes (no appended transcript, at most 32,000 characters), the title and `MeetingSummary.instruction`, and stores the cleaned answer of at most 600 characters as the notes summary. It works the same in both build variants with whatever provider the variant offers. Errors: provider None makes no call; a failed call (the provider is unavailable, the command is missing, the endpoint answers an error), a call cancelled after 180 seconds or an empty answer stores nothing, keeps the earlier notes summary, logs one line without content and shows no notification or job warning; the answer of a call that a newer save of the same meeting overtook, or of a meeting moved to the Trash, is dropped; a quit drops the call, and no call is made for it at the next launch; the call never delays the job or the next job in the queue. [paraphrase]
- **R6:** The All Recordings search matches the summary a row shows as well as the title and the participants, ignoring case and diacritics, and its hint reads "Search titles and summaries". No error surface beyond today's search. [paraphrase]
- **R7:** Move Meeting to Trash removes the meeting's summaries once its files are moved; a deletion left incomplete keeps them until it finishes; Move Audio to Trash and the automatic deletion leave them. At launch, summaries whose job neither the finished-job list nor the record file holds are dropped. Errors: a failed removal is logged without content and retried at the next launch by the same rule. [inferred]
- **R8:** The summary section rule, the answer clean-up, the replace-or-keep rule for every save point of R2 and the menu item list for every job state are pure functions with unit tests: notes from the built-in template with an English and a German summary heading, the summary heading of each notes language the app offers, a custom template without a summary section, a summary section that is a list, an appended full transcript, label lines, a bracketed placeholder, Markdown marks and the 600-character cut. The store is tested with a temporary folder: an unreadable file, two writers, a write failure. The summary call is tested with a stub generator: it is called once with the expected text, title and instruction for notes without a summary section, never for notes with one or with provider None, and a failure, a time-out, an empty answer, an overtaken call and a meeting moved to the Trash each store nothing. The re-run's success step is tested in the pipeline's test setup with a stub generator, with a notes summary and with a typed summary. Each new control has one ViewInspector wiring test located by an `A11yID` constant. Both build variants run the suite, and `docs/architecture/pipeline.md` describes the summary, its file, its rule and the call. No error surface beyond R1 to R7. [inferred]
