# Audio devices window with live levels

## Conversation Evidence

The fork is public, so the owner's turns are paraphrased, not quoted.

## Goal & Context

<!-- Source: 60% [paraphrase] / 40% [inferred] -->

When a recording's track comes out silent, or a call sounds wrong, it is hard to tell which microphone or output the meeting app uses and which one carries sound. The menu bar's Audio Devices section names only the chosen microphone and the Mac's output (gh-148). After this spec, a window **Audio Devices**, opened by a new item "Device Details…" in that section, lists every microphone and every output. Each row says whether the app records it, whether the meeting app uses it, whether any app is running audio on it right now, and whether it is the Mac's default. While a recording runs, the recorded microphone and the recorded meeting audio show their live level as a bar. Switching stays in the menu.

The first build stays within what the app captures already, plus read-only device status from Core Audio. Opening a device only to meter it would show the orange microphone indicator, would switch a Bluetooth headset to its call profile and, during a recording, can stall the app-audio capture (`docs/architecture/recording.md`, start order). Levels for devices the app does not record are therefore a later step the owner can ask for (D3).

## Architecture & Data Models

- **One new window, "Audio Devices"**, a window scene like All Recordings (`Window("All Recordings", id: TranscriptionsView.windowID)` in `MeetingTranscriberApp.swift`), opened by a new last item "Device Details…" in the menu bar's Audio Devices section (`MenuBarView.audioDevicesSection`), under the Microphone and Output entries (D2, D7). Choosing the item opens the window or brings it to the front, as "All Recordings…" does through `bringWindowToFront(id:)`. [paraphrase]
- **The accepted layout and wording** (the boards, D7 to D10):
  - *Menu item:* "Device Details…" with a three-bar level icon, the last item of Audio Devices, no keyboard shortcut. The Microphone and Output entries above it stay as they are.
  - *Window:* titled "Audio Devices". Top to bottom: the heading "Microphones" and its list; under that list, while the Microphone entry shows its hint about the meeting app's microphone, that hint as a grey line, word for word (`MeetingMicrophoneWarningPolicy.hint`, "<App> uses <devices>", for example "Teams uses USB Headset"); the heading "Outputs" and its list; while the recording taps an app, the heading "Meeting audio" and its one row; then a grey footer line. Each list is a rounded box with one row per device and thin lines between rows.
  - *Row:* the device name at the left, then its marks as small capsules, then, only while a recording runs, a level column at the right edge. In that column a recorded row shows the level bar and every other row the grey text "no level". Without a recording the rows have no level column.
  - *Marks,* in this order and with these words: "Recording" (red, with a red dot), "Used by <App>" (blue, for example "Used by Teams"), "In use" (grey), "Default" (outlined). The Meeting audio row is named after the recorded app (for example "Teams") and carries "Recording".
  - *Level bar:* 12 segments in a row, lit segments green, unlit ones grey (A9 maps the level to segments).
  - *Footer:* during a recording "Levels show for what this recording captures. To switch a device, use Microphone or Output in the menu bar."; without a recording "Levels show while a recording runs. To switch a device, use Microphone or Output in the menu bar."
  - *States the boards do not draw* (paused, not connected, unknown, the routing warning, an empty group) take A8's words.
- **One pure value decides what the window shows**, in the way `MicrophoneMenuState` and `OutputMenuState` decide the menu entries: the rows of each group, every mark, every string, and whether a row shows a level bar. The window only renders it, so every arm is tested without the window. [inferred]
- **Its inputs are values the app already holds:** the microphone list and the default input (the list the Microphone entry offers, `MicrophoneController.devices`; the default input's UID comes from `MicInputDevice.systemDefaultInput()`, as the controller keeps only its name today), the output list and the default output (`OutputDeviceController.devices` and `current`), the configured microphone (`AppSettings.micDeviceUID`, for the not-connected row; the "No Microphone" setting is not an input, see Edge Cases), the device the running recording captures (`MicrophoneController.recordedDevice`) and whether its microphone track is active (the recorder's `microphoneTrackActive`), the recorded app's name (`MicrophoneController.Attachment.meetingAppName`), the Microphone entry's hint (`MicrophoneController.meetingAppHint`), whether a recording runs, whether it taps an app (`RecordingSource.capturesAppAudio`) and whether it is paused, and the two recorded levels in dBFS. [inferred]
- **Two readings are new:** per device, Core Audio's "device is running somewhere" flag; and, during a recording that taps an app, which input and output devices the tapped processes use. The meeting microphone probe does not cover the second: it runs only while the microphone is recorded and keeps only its mismatch hint. So the window's reader reads the tapped processes' devices itself (the input side as `MeetingMicrophoneProbe.read` does, plus the output-scope counterpart), on the probe's cadence (A7). [inferred]
- **Levels come only from the running recording** (D3): the microphone track's level on the recorded microphone's row, and the app-audio track's level on its own "Meeting audio" row named after the recorded app (D4). The app-audio tap records the meeting app's sound whatever output it plays to, so that level belongs to the app, not to an output row. `ChannelHealthController` keeps the two levels unobserved on purpose (a bound view would redraw ten times a second for ever), so the window samples them on its own timer while it is visible. [inferred]
- **New readings run off the main thread**, one at a time, only while the window is open, because coreaudiod can stop answering (`docs/architecture/recording.md`, capture-restart bounding). [inferred]

## API Contracts

- The debug `/state` snapshot gains `audioDevicesWindow`: `{ "open": true, "rows": [ { "group": "microphone", "name": "USB Headset", "marks": ["meetingApp", "inUse"], "meetingApp": "Teams", "levelBar": false }, { "group": "meetingAudio", "name": "Teams", "marks": ["recording"], "meetingApp": null, "levelBar": true, "litSegments": 7 } ], "selfRunningInput": false }`. `group` is `microphone`, `output` or `meetingAudio`; `marks` holds any of `recording`, `meetingApp`, `inUse`, `default`, `notConnected`, `routingWarning`, `paused`, `unknown`; `meetingApp` is null unless `marks` holds `meetingApp`; `levelBar` says whether the row shows a level bar and `litSegments` how many of its 12 segments are lit (absent without a bar); `selfRunningInput` is whether Core Audio reports this app's own process running audio input, for the no-capture check: the window's reader reads it (the process-object read `MeetingMicrophoneProbe.read` makes, on the app's own process id) with its other readings, off the main thread and one at a time, and the snapshot reports the reader's last result; null while the window is closed, before the first reading returns and when a reading failed or is overdue. Building the snapshot makes no Core Audio call: `/state` is built on the main actor, and a hanging read there would freeze the app. Not part of the `/v1` automation API. [inferred]
- Two debug actions, `POST /action/openAudioDevices` and `POST /action/closeAudioDevices`, open and close the window like `/action/openSettings` and `/action/closeSettings`, so live checks can reach it without the menu. They share one route case and one handler in an extension file, as the speaker actions share theirs, so `DebugRPCServer.route` gains one branch. Debug RPC only, `#if !APPSTORE` like the server. [inferred]
- No new setting, and no change to `/v1`. [inferred]

## Edge Cases & Constraints

- **No recording running:** no level column and no Meeting audio group; the footer says levels show while a recording runs. Marks other than "Recording" and "Used by <App>" still show. [paraphrase]
- **A microphone-only recording:** no Meeting audio group, since no app is recorded; the recorded microphone's row shows its bar. [inferred]
- **A recording that does not capture the microphone** (an app-only recording, started with "No Microphone (app audio only)" on, or one whose microphone track gave up): no microphone row is marked as recorded and no microphone bar shows; the Meeting audio row still does. [inferred]
- **The running recording decides, never the setting:** a microphone row's "Recording" mark and bar follow the device the recording reports it captures while its microphone track is active. "No Microphone" takes effect only at the next recording start, so turning it on or off during a recording changes neither that recording's marks nor its bars. The microphone list stays shown with "No Microphone" on. [inferred]
- **Recording paused:** bars show no lit segment, and the Meeting audio row says "Paused" (A8). [inferred]
- **A device with both input and output** (a USB headset) appears once in each group. [paraphrase]
- **The app's own capture counts as running:** the recorded microphone and the output the app-audio tap is built on report "running" because of this app. So the `inUse` mark, shown as "In use", says only that some app, this one included, runs audio on the device; no string claims "another app". [paraphrase]
- **A configured microphone that is not connected** is listed in the microphone group as not connected, as in the Microphone entry; the Mac's default output that the Mac does not offer (the Teams device) is listed with the menu's routing warning (both worded by A8). [inferred]
- **A failed or late read** shows the mark `unknown` on that row, never "not running": "not in use" and "could not ask" must stay apart. A read that has not returned within two seconds leaves the window responsive and its rows `unknown`. [inferred]
- **Devices come and go:** the lists refresh while the window is open when a device is added or removed or a default changes, as the menu entries do. [inferred]
- **Privacy:** the window shows device names and the meeting app's name, never the meeting title and never a device UID (a USB UID carries the serial number). An output whose name Core Audio did not return is listed as "Unnamed device" in the window and in `/state`, because `OutputDevices.device` then uses its UID as its name. Nothing new is logged. Others may see the window during a screen share. [inferred]
- **Both build variants** get the window; a read the App Store sandbox refuses shows as `unknown`. [inferred]

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1:** The menu bar's Audio Devices section ends with the item "Device Details…" (a three-bar level icon, no keyboard shortcut), which opens the Audio Devices window, or brings it to the front when it is open. The window's layout and wording are the accepted boards' (Architecture, the accepted layout and wording; D7 to D10), and no row holds a control. Errors: the item shows exactly when the section shows; no second window opens on a second choice. [paraphrase]
- **R2:** The window lists every microphone of the Microphone entry's list (`MicrophoneController.devices`, also while "No Microphone" is on) and every output the Output entry offers, under the headings "Microphones" and "Outputs" and in the menus' order, each by its name, and refreshes while open when a device is added or removed or a default changes. Errors: an empty group shows one "None found" row; a configured microphone that is not connected is listed as not connected; a device with both input and output appears in both groups; an output without a readable name is listed as "Unnamed device", never by its UID, in the window and in `/state`. [paraphrase]
- **R3:** Each row shows its marks, in the order "Recording", "Used by <App>", "In use", "Default": `recording` on the microphone the running recording reports it captures while its microphone track is active, and on the Meeting audio row; `meetingApp`, shown as "Used by <App>", on a microphone or an output the tapped meeting app uses during a recording; `inUse` while Core Audio reports the device running for any process; `default` on the Mac's default input and default output. When the Microphone entry shows its hint about the meeting app's microphone, the window shows the same text as a line under the Microphones list. Errors: a failed or late read shows `unknown` on that row, never "not running"; outside a recording no row carries `recording` or `meetingApp`. [paraphrase]
- **R4:** While a recording runs and is not paused, the recorded microphone's row and, while the recording taps an app, a "Meeting audio" row named after the recorded app show a live 12-segment level bar from the recording's microphone and app-audio tracks (A9), updated at least ten times a second while the window is visible; every other row shows "no level" in the level column, and the footer reads "Levels show for what this recording captures. To switch a device, use Microphone or Output in the menu bar." Errors: no recording → no level column, no Meeting audio group, and the footer "Levels show while a recording runs. To switch a device, use Microphone or Output in the menu bar."; a recording that does not capture the microphone (app-only, or its microphone track gave up) → no microphone bar; "No Microphone" turned on or off during a recording → that recording's marks and bars unchanged; a microphone-only recording → no Meeting audio group; paused → no lit segment and the Meeting audio row says "Paused"; a track that stops delivering → its bar falls to no lit segment within one second. [paraphrase]
- **R5:** Opening, showing and closing the window opens no audio input, installs no audio tap and starts no capture, with or without a recording: the window adds no orange microphone indicator and changes no Bluetooth headset's profile. Errors: no error surface beyond R3's `unknown`. [paraphrase]
- **R6:** The new Core Audio readings run off the main thread, at most one at a time, only while the window is open, and stop when it closes; the window stays responsive while a reading hangs. Errors: a reading that has not returned within two seconds → the rows it would answer show `unknown` until a later reading returns. [inferred]
- **R7:** The debug `/state` snapshot carries `audioDevicesWindow` as in API Contracts, built from the window's state and its reader's last results with no Core Audio call of its own, and `docs/architecture-macos.md` lists the window and where it gets each mark and level. Errors: window closed → `open: false` and `rows: []`. [inferred]
