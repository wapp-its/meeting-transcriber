## Goal & Context

When a detected meeting is over, the person recording wants to end its recording themselves, from the menu, instead of waiting until the app notices the call has ended (the end grace of 15 s, then gh-54's 2-minute question). Teams often keeps its window or the microphone after a meeting, so that wait can be long. After such a stop the same call must not start a new recording or a new prompt while its app still looks like it is in a call; the next real call is recorded as usual. [paraphrase]

What the code does today (checked on `wapp/main` at 744e8e81): the menu scene passes the "Stop Recording" closure to `MenuBarView` only when `AppState.isManualRecording`; during a detected meeting the menu offers only "Stop Watching for Meetings", which ends the recording and also switches watching off. A detected meeting ends inside `WatchLoop.waitForMeetingEnd`: the duration cap, gh-54's question (countdown, "Stop now", "Keep recording") or cancellation by Stop Watching. After a recording the loop goes straight back to polling, and the detectors' cooldown after a meeting is 5 s, so a call signal that is still there is detected again within seconds. [inferred]

The automation API's `/v1/record` `stop` documents the opposite of the issue's third wish: "This endpoint only ever stops the recording it could have started" and `recording` is "deliberately narrower … so a client is never invited to `stop` a recording it did not start" (`docs/automation-api.md`, `POST /v1/record` and `RecordStatusDTO`). Changing what `stop` means would break that `/v1` stability promise, so the owner chose an opt-in instead (decision D3): `{"action":"stop","scope":"any"}` ends whatever is recording, and a plain `stop` keeps its documented meaning. Today a Stream Deck key can end a detected recording only through `POST /v1/watch` `stop`, which also switches watching off. [inferred]

## Architecture & Data Models

- **One stop entry point for the menu.** `WatchingController` gets `stopRecording()` in a new extension file: a manual recording stops through the existing `stopManualRecording()` (unchanged); a detected meeting's recording is asked to stop through the loop (below); nothing recording does nothing. `AppState` gets a single-member accessor `canStopRecording` (true while the watch loop's state is `.recording`, any kind of recording), and the menu scene passes the stop closure to `MenuBarView` whenever it is true. `MenuBarView` itself needs no new behaviour: it already shows "Stop Recording" whenever its stop closure is set. [inferred]
- **The stop request (WatchLoop).** `WatchLoop.stopDetectedRecording()` parks a request, stamped with the time it was made, when a detected meeting is recording and returns whether it was accepted; a repeat while one is parked keeps the first stamp. `waitForMeetingEnd` checks the request at the start of every poll, before gh-54's pure `WatchLoopEndPolicy.step` runs, and when one is parked ends the wait at once: it writes the diagnostic line, places the re-detection hold, and returns the cut point gh-54 already uses when watching stops (`WatchLoopEndPolicy.cutWhenWatchingStops`, nil unless gh-54's question is open), given only a parked answer that arrived before the request's stamp, so a "Keep recording" tapped after "Stop Recording" cannot keep the countdown minutes. `handleMeeting` then stops, (cuts,) and enqueues exactly as for every other end, and the loop returns to watching. The request is cleared whenever `handleMeeting` exits, so it can never end a later recording. The new logic lives in a new `WatchLoop+StopByHand.swift`; `WatchLoopEndPolicy` gets no new input. [inferred]
- **Re-detection hold (reusable, for gh-45 too).** A pure value type `RedetectionHolds` keeps, per app (keyed by `meeting.pattern.appName`, the identity the consent exclusion already passes to `checkOnce(excluding:)`), the `DetectedMeeting` whose signal must disappear before that app is detected again. `WatchLoop` holds one instance; a new `WatchLoop+RedetectionHold.swift` adds `holdRedetection(of:)`, `releaseEndedRedetectionHolds()` and `appsHeldFromDetection`. At the top of every watching poll, `watchLoop()` releases each hold whose own meeting reports `!detector.isMeetingActive(meeting)`, then passes the consent exclusion plus the held apps to `detector.checkOnce(excluding:)`, so a held app is neither recorded nor asked about; a held app's approved consent meeting is not started either. `stop()` clears all holds. gh-45 (ask to stop after long silence) should call the same `holdRedetection(of:)` after its silence stop instead of building its own hold; its task .4 then only adds that call and its own tests. [inferred]
- **Automation API opt-in.** `RecordActionPayload` gains an optional `scope`, whose only value is `any`, accepted only together with `action: "stop"`; any other `scope` value, or `scope` with `start`/`toggle` (or a verb added later), fails to decode, which the route already answers with 400. The route sends a `scope: "any"` stop to a seam of its own (`WatchingController.applyRecordStopAny()`, reached through a separate server closure so the existing `RecordAction` closure and its callers stay as they are); a plain `stop` takes today's path unchanged. The stop-any settles in-flight starts like every record action, then stops whatever records through the menu's `stopRecording()`, and for a detected meeting waits, within the API's existing 20 s bound, until the loop has left `.recording`, so the response body describes the state after the stop. Outcomes map onto the existing codes: nothing recording → `unchanged` (200), stopped and handed on → `changed` (200), stop lost the recording or did not settle → `failed` (503). The watch loop outlives its recordings, so a detected meeting's failure is judged on that recording alone: starting a recording clears the loop's last error, and the verdict (still recording, error state, or an error recorded since the stop) is one helper shared with the plain microphone stop, so two meetings failing with the same message both answer 503. [inferred]
- **Diagnostic lines** through the loop's existing `diagnostics` sink (gh-54): `recording_stopped_by_hand trigger=auto` when a detected meeting is stopped by hand, `redetect_hold_set app=<appName>` and `redetect_hold_released app=<appName>`. The app name is the detector identity (e.g. "Microsoft Teams", "Google Chrome"), already logged publicly by the consent code; no meeting title, participant or transcript text. The line does not start with `recording_auto_stop`: gh-54's tests pin that a stop by hand is not an automatic stop. [inferred]

## Edge Cases & Constraints

- **Stop while gh-54's "Meeting seems to have ended" question is open:** the question is withdrawn (the wait's existing `defer`), and the recording ends as Stop Watching ends it in that state (gh-54 R6): cut back to where the signal was lost plus the end grace, unless "Keep recording" was answered before "Stop Recording" (and before the countdown's deadline). The order of the two taps counts even when both fall between the same two polls: Keep then Stop keeps everything, Stop then Keep cuts. Stopped after "Keep recording" took effect, or while the signal is there or missing for less than the end grace, nothing is cut. [inferred]
- **Latency:** the request is acted on at the next poll of the meeting-end wait, up to one poll interval (3 s by default, 1–30 s in Settings), the same as gh-54's "Stop now". Until then the menu still shows the recording; choosing "Stop Recording" again changes nothing and keeps the first request's time. [inferred]
- **Recorder fails to stop:** the loop reports the error as for any recording whose stop throws (error state, the controller's error notification, no job) and returns to watching after its usual pause; the hold was placed before the stop, so the still-signalling app is not recorded again. [inferred]
- **Stop chosen while the recorder is still starting** (the loop shows `.recording` before capture runs): the first poll of the wait ends the recording. If capture fails to start, the request is dropped with the failed recording and never ends a later one. [inferred]
- **A stop request when no detected meeting records** (watching, idle, a manual recording) is refused and changes nothing; manual recordings keep stopping through `stopManualRecording()`. [inferred]
- **Stop Watching, the duration cap, or gh-54's countdown arriving in the same poll as a stop request:** Stop Watching cancels the wait and ends the recording as it does today (no hold, the loop is discarded); otherwise the request is checked first and wins. [inferred]
- **Signal already gone when stopped:** the hold is placed and released at the next watching poll. [inferred]
- **Holds are checked only while watching polls.** While another app's meeting records, a held app is not looked at; if its call ends and a new one starts entirely within that recording, the app stays held until its signal is next seen gone. The signal disappearing is the only boundary the detector has, so a new call in the same app that follows the old call state without a gap of at least one poll is not recorded; the hold lines in the diagnostic log show it, and a manual recording or Stop/Start Watching (which discards all holds) records it. [inferred]
- **Several apps:** each held app has its own hold, released only by its own signal disappearing; a second stop by hand adds a hold and never releases the first. Other apps' meetings are detected and recorded as usual during a hold. [inferred]
- **Record-only mode:** a stopped detected meeting writes its WAVs and sidecar as a meeting end does; the sidecar's `trigger` stays `auto` (who started the recording, not who stopped it), and its `stoppedAt` is the stop (or gh-54's cut point when the question was open). [inferred]
- **Holds live in memory only:** quitting the app, or Stop Watching and Start Watching, forgets them; a call still running then is detected as a new meeting and recorded or asked about as usual. [inferred]
- The watch loop's and the watching controller's main files sit near SwiftLint's 600-line `file_length` limit (lint runs `--strict`), so the new logic goes into extension files; the menu view keeps the label of its stop closure, which other planned specs construct the view with. [inferred]
- `#if !APPSTORE`: nothing here is variant-specific; both variants get the menu item and the hold. [inferred]

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1:** While any recording runs (a detected meeting, an app recording started from the menu, a microphone-only recording), the menu shows "Stop Recording" (shortcut ".") in place of "Record Microphone Only" and "Record App …". Errors: in the moment between registering a manual start and its recording running, the item is not shown, as today. [paraphrase]
- **R2:** "Stop Recording" on a detected meeting's recording ends the recording at the next detection poll (within the poll interval, 3 s by default) and processes it exactly as a meeting end does (one job: transcript and protocol, or in record-only mode the WAVs and sidecar), keeping all audio up to the stop; meeting watching stays on and the menu's status returns to "Watching for Meetings...". Errors: if the recorder fails to stop, the failure is reported as for any recording that fails to stop (error state and notification), watching continues, and R4's hold still applies. [paraphrase]
- **R3:** When "Stop Recording" is chosen while gh-54's "Meeting seems to have ended" question is open, the question is withdrawn and the recording ends as Stop Watching ends it in that state: cut back to where the signal was lost plus the end grace, unless "Keep recording" was answered before the stop. Errors: a "Keep recording" or "Stop now" tapped after "Stop Recording", before the next poll, does not change the cut; an answer to the withdrawn question changes nothing; a failed cut is handled as gh-54 specifies (processed uncut, logged). [inferred]
- **R4:** After "Stop Recording" ended a detected meeting's recording, that app's meeting is neither recorded nor asked about ("Record … meeting?") again while its call signal is still present. The first watching poll that finds the app's signal gone releases the hold, and the app's next call is then detected, asked about and recorded as usual. Other apps' meetings are detected and recorded as usual during a hold; each app's hold is independent; Stop Watching discards all holds. Errors: none beyond the detector's own. [paraphrase]
- **R5:** A stop by hand of a detected meeting, and every hold placed and released, writes one line to the diagnostic log the app keeps on disk: `recording_stopped_by_hand trigger=auto`, `redetect_hold_set app=<app>`, `redetect_hold_released app=<app>`. No line carries a meeting title, participant name or transcript content. Errors: none beyond the log write itself. [inferred]
- **R6:** Manual recordings stop through "Stop Recording" exactly as today, and the existing automation verbs keep their documented behaviour: `POST /v1/record` `stop` without `scope` still ends only a microphone-only recording (any other recording → 200, left alone), `toggle` stays microphone-only, and `POST /v1/watch` `stop` still stops watching. Errors: none. [inferred]
- **R7:** `POST /v1/record` with `{"action":"stop","scope":"any"}` ends whatever recording runs: a detected meeting exactly as "Stop Recording" in the menu does (R2–R5: watching stays on, the app is held from re-detection), an app or microphone-only recording as a plain stop of it does. The response is the usual `RecordStatusDTO` describing the state after the stop: 200 when a recording was stopped and handed to processing, 200 unchanged when nothing was recording. `docs/automation-api.md` documents the option next to `stop` and keeps the existing promise for a plain `stop`; `docs/stream-deck.md` shows a key that ends any recording. Errors: an unknown `scope` value, or `scope` with any action other than `stop`, → 400 with an empty body and nothing applied (never treated as `any`); a stop whose recording could not be handed on (the recorder or the record-only write failed), or a detected meeting's stop that has not taken effect within 20 s, → 503 with the status body. [paraphrase]
