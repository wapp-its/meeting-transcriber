## Goal & Context

When someone records a meeting, their own voice ends up far quieter than the other participants in the recording they listen to afterwards, about 20 to 25 dB in the owner's measurements, even with the right headset. The person wants both sides at a similar loudness, within about 6 dB, in the saved mixed recording and in the voice samples the speaker-naming dialog plays, with a switch to turn the balancing off. [paraphrase]

Today the app writes three files per meeting: the far end (`_app.wav`), the microphone (`_mic.wav`) and a mix of the two (`_mix.wav`), made by `AudioMixer.mix` when the recording stops (and again by crash recovery after a crash). The mix is what a person opens to listen to a meeting, in the recordings folder and in the record-only output. Transcription, diarization, the echo-bleed detector and echo cancellation never read the mix of a dual-source recording: they read the per-track files. The only part of the pipeline that reads the mix of a dual-source recording is the naming dialog's fallback sample source (`<slug>_16k.wav`, a resampled copy of the mix). Since PR #56 the naming dialog plays each speaker's sample from that speaker's own 16 kHz track (`SpeakerNamingData.sampleSource`), so a quiet microphone track gives a quiet own-voice sample. [inferred]

The fix balances only what a person listens to: each track is brought to a common speech level before mixing, and each naming-dialog sample is brought to the same level before it plays. The per-track files, and with them everything the pipeline computes, stay exactly as recorded. [inferred]

## Architecture & Data Models

- **`LevelBalance` (new pure type).** No I/O, no settings, no actor. [inferred]
  - *Speech level estimate.* Split the samples into consecutive 100 ms frames and take each frame's RMS in dBFS. Frames at or below −90 dBFS (digital silence, gated stretches, alignment padding) are ignored. The noise floor is the 10th percentile of the remaining frame levels. A frame counts as speech when its level is at or above the larger of −60 dBFS and noise floor + 10 dB. The speech level is the power mean of the speech frames, in dBFS (10·log10 of the mean of their mean-square values). When the speech frames add up to less than the minimum speech duration (5 s for a whole track, 0.5 s for a naming sample), the level is "not measurable". [inferred]
  - *Gain.* Target speech level −20 dBFS. Gain = target − measured level, at most +24 dB, with no lower bound (a cut is always safe). Not measurable means a gain of 0 dB. The gain is then lowered, if needed, until at most 0.1 % of the samples inside the track's speech frames would exceed full scale after it (this only ever lowers a boost, never below 0 dB); every sample that still exceeds full scale is clamped to ±1.0. [inferred]
  - *Outcome.* The estimate and gain functions return a small value type (measured level or not measurable, applied gain in dB, whether the +24 dB cap or the clip budget lowered it), so tests and the log line read the same numbers. [inferred]
- **`AudioMixer.mix`** gains a `levelBalance: Bool = false` parameter. Order inside `mix` when it is true: load both tracks, clamp the delay, run `suppressEcho` exactly as today (the gate is decided from the *unbalanced* app track), then measure the app track and the gated microphone track, apply each track's gain in place, write the log line, then align and average with `mixTracks` as today. When false, the function is today's code path unchanged. [inferred]
- **Setting.** `AppSettings.levelBalanceEnabled: Bool`, UserDefaults key `levelBalanceEnabled`, default `true`. Shown in Settings → Audio as its own section with one `HelpfulToggle` (`A11yID.levelBalanceToggle`, help text `SettingsHelp.levelBalance`). Not dimmed in record-only mode, because record-only writes the mix too. [inferred]
- **Recording wiring.** `DualSourceRecorder` gets a `var levelBalanceEnabled = false`, set per recording by the recorder factory from the setting, exactly like `silentTrackWatchdogEnabled`. `stop()` hands it to `buildRecording(…, levelBalance:)`, which passes it to `AudioMixer.mix`. Crash recovery (`recoverCrashedRecordings` → `recoverCrashedRecording` → `buildRecording`) takes the same flag as a parameter. `PipelineController` reaches that recovery through its queue environment's staged-recording recovery callback, which today receives only the new queue: the callback gains the flag as a second argument, the controller passes `settings.levelBalanceEnabled` when it builds a queue (on the main actor), and the production callback hands it into its detached recovery task. [inferred]
- **Naming-dialog samples.** `SpeakerNamingView` gets an init parameter `balanceSampleLevels: Bool = false`; `MeetingTranscriberApp.speakerNamingForm` passes `appState.settings.levelBalanceEnabled`. `playSpeakerSnippet` builds the cut sample through a new pure static helper that, when the flag is on, runs `LevelBalance` over the cut sample (0.5 s minimum) before writing the temporary WAV. This covers every source `sampleSource` can pick (app track, microphone track, or the mix fallback). [inferred]
- **Untouched on purpose.** `_app.wav`, `_mic.wav`, the pipeline's `app_16k.wav`/`mic_16k.wav` and their `_app_16k.wav`/`_mic_16k.wav` sidecars, `suppressEcho`, `mixTracks`, `EchoBleedDetector`, echo cancellation, `PipelineQueue.ensureMixAudio` (the temporary mix it builds for a paired import without a `_mix.wav` calls `mix` without the flag; for a recording with a `_mix.wav` it only resamples that file), the `RecordingSidecar` format, and `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md` (fork rule: those belong to the original). [inferred]

## Edge Cases & Constraints

- **Single-track recordings** (microphone-only, app-only, or one track failed): the mix is the one track as today; there is nothing to balance. [inferred]
- **A track with no measurable speech** (digital zeros, a muted or disconnected microphone, steady fan noise, under 5 s of speech): mixed at its recorded level, never boosted. The other track is still brought to the target. [paraphrase]
- **Loudspeakers instead of a headset:** the far end leaks into the microphone. Because the gate runs first and the microphone is measured after it, the leaked far end does not count as own voice. Leftover echo that the gate does not catch is raised together with the own voice. [inferred]
- **Talking over each other:** the gate keeps muting the microphone in the mix while the far end speaks, exactly as today; this spec changes levels, not the gate (see Boundaries). [inferred]
- **Built-in microphone and typing:** the estimate is energy-based. A person who never speaks but types near the built-in microphone can have the typing measured as their speech level and raised by up to 24 dB. Accepted for this slice; the switch turns it off. [inferred]
- **Noise floor:** a boost raises the track's noise with the voice (up to 24 dB). That is the cost of a quiet microphone; the cap bounds it. [inferred]
- **Transient-heavy microphone** (desk knocks, a headset mic brushing clothing): the clip budget can stop the boost short of the target, so such a recording can stay more than 6 dB apart; the log line says the clip budget applied. Accepted over audible clipping. [inferred]
- **Mix-only re-import:** a `_mix.wav` imported without its two track files becomes a single-source job that is transcribed from the mix, so a balanced mix reaches transcription on that path only. Accepted: every recording the app makes, and every paired re-import, transcribes the per-track files. [inferred]
- **Record-only mode:** the balanced `_mix.wav` goes to the output folder; the unchanged `_app.wav` and `_mic.wav` go next to it, so a downstream processor can still use the raw tracks. [inferred]
- **Existing recordings** are not re-mixed. A naming dialog still open from before the update plays balanced samples, because sample balancing happens at playback. [inferred]
- **Long recordings** (up to the 4-hour cap): the analysis is two passes over audio `mix` already holds in memory, and the gain is applied in place, so no extra full-length copy is made. [inferred]
- **Mic delay:** gains are applied before alignment; the zero padding stays zero and the alignment is unchanged. [inferred]
- **App Store variant:** pure audio code and a setting; no `#if APPSTORE` branch. [inferred]

### Verification

- `LevelBalance` unit tests on synthetic signals (tone or noise bursts with pauses, at known levels): the estimate within 1 dB of the bursts' level whether speech fills 50 % of a 20 s track or 5 % of a 120 s track (6 s, above the 5 s minimum); a 60 s track with 3 s of speech is not measurable; not measurable for digital zeros, steady noise and under-minimum speech; the +24 dB cap; the 0.1 % clip budget on a track with a few loud transients, including one where speech fills only 5 % of the track; the clamp. [inferred]
- `AudioMixer.mix` tests with two synthetic tracks: app bursts at about −18 dBFS and microphone bursts at about −44 dBFS, not overlapping. With `levelBalance: true` the two sides' speech levels in the mix are within 6 dB; with `false` the output is sample-identical to `mixTracks` over the `suppressEcho`'d inputs (today's behaviour). A gate test: microphone samples under app energy are still zero in the balanced mix. [inferred]
- Wiring tests: the setting's default, persistence and toggle write-back (pattern `Tests/SilentTrackWatchdogSettingTests.swift`); `buildRecording` with fixture files writes a balanced mix when the flag is on and leaves `_app.wav`/`_mic.wav` byte-identical either way; the setting reaches a recording the controller starts (pattern `DualSourceRecorderLifecycleTests.testTheSettingReachesARecordingTheControllerStarts`); crash recovery honours the flag. [inferred]
- Naming playback helper tests: a quiet sample comes out at the target level, a loud one is lowered, an unmeasurable one is unchanged, and the flag off returns the cut unchanged. [inferred]
- Full suite, lint with the pinned tools, and `./scripts/pre-push.sh --with-appstore`. [inferred]
- Owner check after merge (not automatable): a real headset meeting and a built-in-microphone meeting, listened to in `_mix.wav` and in the naming dialog, plus the log line's gains. [inferred]

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1:** With the setting on, every dual-source recording's saved mix (`_mix.wav` in the recordings folder and in the record-only output, including one rebuilt by crash recovery) has the own voice and the far end at speech levels within 6 dB of each other, whenever each track carries at least 5 s of measurable speech and can reach −20 dBFS within both limits of R2 (the +24 dB cap and the clip budget). When a limit stops a track short, that track is raised exactly as far as the limit allows, the log line names the limit (R6), and the 6 dB promise does not hold for that recording. Errors: a track whose speech is not measurable is mixed at its recorded level and never boosted; balancing never stops the mix from being written or the recording from being processed. [paraphrase]
- **R2:** No track is boosted by more than 24 dB, a boost is lowered so that at most 0.1 % of the samples in a track's speech frames would exceed full scale, every sample that still would is clamped, and the mix never exceeds full scale. Errors: none beyond R1. [inferred]
- **R3:** Transcription, diarization, the echo-bleed detector, echo cancellation and speaker recognition read the same audio whether the setting is on or off: `_app.wav` and `_mic.wav` (and every 16 kHz copy made from them) are byte-identical either way, and the echo gate silences the same microphone windows as today. Errors: none; a difference is a test failure. [paraphrase]
- **R4:** With the setting on, each voice sample played in the speaker-naming dialog, from the speaker's own track or from the mix fallback, is brought to −20 dBFS speech level under the same cap and clip rules, so own-voice and far-end samples play within 6 dB of each other. Errors: a sample with under 0.5 s of measurable speech plays unchanged; playback stays best-effort as today. [paraphrase]
- **R5:** Settings → Audio shows a switch for the balancing, on by default, that survives a relaunch. With it off, recordings started afterwards get a mix sample-identical to today's mixer output, and naming samples play unchanged. The switch is read when a recording starts, so a change during a recording applies from the next one; crash recovery at launch uses the switch's value at that launch. Errors: none beyond R1. [paraphrase]
- **R6:** Each mix made with balancing on writes one diagnostic log line at a retained (notice or higher) level with each track's measured speech level (or "not measurable") and applied gain, and whether the cap or the clip budget lowered it. The line carries no file name, meeting title, participant name or audio content. Errors: none beyond the log write itself. [inferred]

## Early proof point

Task gh-4-balance-own-voice-and-remote-levels.1 validates the core approach (the speech-level estimate and capped gain bring a −44 dBFS microphone and a −18 dBFS far end to within 6 dB in the mix, leave unmeasurable tracks alone, and keep the echo gate's decisions unchanged). If it fails, re-evaluate the estimator (frame gating and power mean) before continuing with gh-4-balance-own-voice-and-remote-levels.2+.

## Boundaries

- Out (moved to issue #43's spec by the coordinator): showing which microphone records and warning when it is not the meeting app's input device. [paraphrase]
- Out (done in PR #56): playing each naming sample from the speaker's own track. [paraphrase]
- Out: changing what transcription, diarization, the echo-bleed detector or echo cancellation see, including balancing the per-track files. If transcription quality on quiet microphones becomes a concern, that is a separate owner decision. [paraphrase]
- Out, follow-up: changing the echo gate (`suppressEcho`), for example skipping it on headset recordings so own voice is not muted while the far end talks. [inferred]
- Out, follow-up: a model-based speech detector (the app's Silero VAD) for the level estimate, so typing or rustling is not taken for speech. [inferred]
- Out, follow-up: a live per-source level meter during recording (issue #4 wish; the existing per-channel indicator only flags silence). [paraphrase]
- Out, follow-up: recording the applied gains in the record-only sidecar or on `GET /v1/jobs/<id>`. [inferred]
- Out: re-mixing recordings made before this change, voice-enrollment samples in Settings → Speakers, and the temporary mix the pipeline builds for paired imports. [inferred]
- Out: a target-level or cap setting; only the on/off switch is exposed. [inferred]
