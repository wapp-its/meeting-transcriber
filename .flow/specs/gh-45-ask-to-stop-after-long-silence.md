# Ask to stop after long silence

## Conversation Evidence

## Goal & Context

A recording that nobody stops keeps recording silence: a detected meeting whose app still signals a call after the meeting is over, or a manual recording someone forgot. The person recording wants to be asked, after a stretch in which nothing was said, whether to stop; stopping should trim the silent tail so the transcript and protocol end where the talking ended, and not answering must never lose anything. [paraphrase]

What the code does today (checked on `wapp/main` at 65888b53): `SilentRecordingMonitor` latches `ChannelHealthController.recordingSilentActive` when both channels sit at or below -60 dBFS for the "Warn after" time (`AppSettings.asymmetricSilenceWarningSeconds`, default 90 s). That tints the whole menu-bar icon and also posts one "Recording Appears Silent" notification, which is written for a broken capture (check the input device, exclusive-mode headsets). So the issue's "only tints" is slightly out of date, but nothing ever asks to stop. The menu's "Stop Recording" exists only for manual recordings (`MeetingTranscriberApp.swift:144`, `WatchLoop.stopManualRecording`); a detected meeting ends through meeting-end detection or "Stop Watching". [inferred]

This spec builds on gh-54 ("Ask before auto-stopping a meeting recording", issue #54), which is planned and being built but not merged yet. gh-54 brings the pieces this spec reuses: a notification category with the actions "Keep recording" and "Stop now" whose answers arrive out of band and are matched to the open question by id, a cut that ends every saved track at one point on the recording's timeline (all or nothing), a record-only sidecar whose stop time follows the cut, the meeting-end poll loop with an open question, and an auto-stop line in the diagnostic log. Build this spec only after gh-54 is merged into `wapp/main`. [inferred]

## Architecture & Data Models

- **Silence clock (fact source).** `ChannelHealthController` already polls both channel levels at about 10 Hz during every recording and stamps, per channel, the last tick whose level reached the speech threshold (`lastSpeechAt`, threshold `ChannelHealthMonitor.speechThresholdDBFS` = -50 dBFS). It gains one read-only accessor that reports, for the recording in progress, when the poll first observed it and the latest speech tick on any channel the recording opened (nil when no speech has been heard yet); nil before the first tick. No new polling and no new threshold. [inferred]
- **Decision (pure).** A new value type `SilencePromptPolicy` decides, once per poll, from: the configured time (nil when switched off), the current time, the silence clock, a `held` flag (another question is open), the answer parked since the last poll, and its own small state (whether a question is open and for which silence, and the moment from which silence counts toward the next question). It returns one action: nothing, ask (with the silence length), withdraw (with a reason), or stop (with the cut point, nil for "do not cut"). [inferred]
- **Owner of the question.** `WatchLoop` runs the policy from the poll loops it already runs during every recording: `monitorManualRecording` for manual recordings and gh-54's `waitForMeetingEnd` for detected ones. It posts and withdraws the question through gh-54's meeting-end notifier calls under a fresh id per question, parks an answer only when it belongs to the open question, and withdraws an open question on every way a recording ends. The logic lives in a new `WatchLoop+SilencePrompt.swift`; its state is one stored struct on `WatchLoop`. [inferred]
- **Stop.** "Stop now" ends a manual recording through the same stop-and-enqueue path as the menu's "Stop Recording", and a detected one through the meeting-end path (watching continues), each with the cut applied by gh-54's helper at "last speech + 10 s" on the recording's timeline, anchored like gh-54's detected-meeting cut (the loop clock read right after the recorder starts). Record-only recordings get gh-54's sidecar stop time. [inferred]
- **Re-detection hold.** After a silence stop on a detected meeting, `WatchLoop` keeps that meeting's app out of detection (the same `checkOnce(excluding:)` exclusion the consent prompt uses) until a poll finds `detector.isMeetingActive` false for it once. Holds are kept per app: a silence stop of a second app adds a second hold and never replaces the first, and each is released only by its own app's signal disappearing. [inferred]
- **Settings.** `AppSettings.silencePromptEnabled` (default true) and `AppSettings.silencePromptMinutes` (default 5, clamped to 1…60), read on every poll through a closure that `WatchingController` passes to both `WatchLoop` constructions, the way `recordOnly` is passed. UI: a small section view in Settings → General. [paraphrase]

## Edge Cases & Constraints

- Speech heard again while a question is open: the question is withdrawn (R3). If "Stop now" was tapped after the speech but before the next poll, the stop still happens (an explicit answer wins), and the cut point is taken from the newer last speech, so no speech heard before the stop is ever cut away. When that point (last speech + 10 s) is not earlier than the stop itself, nothing is cut, so neither the tracks nor the record-only sidecar ever claim audio past the recording's real end. [inferred]
- A recording in which no speech was heard at all is stopped uncut: there is nothing to trim towards, and if the level measure was wrong for this setup the whole recording would otherwise be lost. [inferred]
- The question cannot be posted or is not shown (notifications denied, alert style None, Focus without time-sensitive): no answer arrives, the recording continues, and the next full silent period posts again; the notifier already logs each drop. [inferred]
- The duration cap, the monitored app quitting, "Stop Watching", the menu's "Stop Recording", or gh-54's countdown ending the recording while a silence question is open: the recording ends exactly as it would without this spec, uncut by this spec, and the silence question is withdrawn. [inferred]
- The setting switched off while a question is open withdraws it at the next poll; a changed minute value applies from the next poll. [inferred]
- Mic-only and app-only recordings: only the channel the recording opened counts; an unopened channel reads -120 dBFS and must not hold the clock. [inferred]
- Detected meeting: a new call in the same app while that app's call signal never disappeared after a silence stop is not recorded; the signal dropping is the only boundary the detector has. Holds are checked by the watching poll, so a held app whose signal disappears and returns entirely while another meeting is being recorded stays held. [inferred]
- A Mac that sleeps during a recording writes no audio while asleep, so a wall-clock cut point maps to the same or a later file position; sleep can only make the cut keep more, never cut speech. [inferred]
- A question left in Notification Center by an earlier app run (quit or crash) changes nothing when answered: no open question matches its id (gh-54's registry). [inferred]
- The cut's start anchor is read after capture starts (gh-54's choice), so it errs towards keeping slightly less; the 10 s margin absorbs that bias together with the 100 ms poll granularity and a soft last word below the threshold. [inferred]
- `WatchLoop.swift` sits near SwiftLint's 600-line `file_length` limit and lint runs `--strict`; new logic goes into the extension file. Settings UI goes into its own small view struct, because the build treats a view body over 300 ms of type-checking as an error (spec gh-17). [inferred]
- `#if !APPSTORE`: nothing here is variant-specific; both variants get the feature. [inferred]

### Verification

- `SilencePromptPolicy` unit tests drive every transition with explicit dates (ask, re-ask, withdraw on speech, keep, held, switched off, stop with and without a cut point) without any sleeping. [inferred]
- `ChannelHealthController` tests feed levels through `applyTick` with a mock recorder and check the silence clock for dual, mic-only and app-only recordings and its reset on `stop()`. [inferred]
- `WatchLoop` tests with `TestClock`, `MockRecorder` and a notifier double that records posted and withdrawn questions and can answer them cover manual and detected recordings end to end, including the re-detection hold and the interplay with gh-54's question. [inferred]
- WAV-fixture tests check that a silence stop leaves every saved track ending at the same timeline point and that a recording without any speech, or a failed cut, is processed uncut. [inferred]
- `AppSettings` tests (default, persistence, clamping) and one ViewInspector wiring test per control. [inferred]
- A live check on real hardware confirms the question appears after a silent stretch in a quiet room, stays away while people talk, and that typing at the desk does not keep resetting it; this is a check for the owner, not a task. [inferred]

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1:** During any recording (manual or detected, record-only included), when no track the recording opened has carried speech for the configured time (default 5 minutes), the app posts one notification titled "Nothing heard for 5 minutes" (the configured or elapsed whole minutes, "1 minute" in the singular) with the body "Stop the recording? Without an answer, it keeps recording." and the actions "Keep recording" and "Stop now". Speech means a level at or above the app's existing speech threshold (-50 dBFS) on the channel-health poll. Errors: a notification that cannot be posted or is not shown changes nothing; the recording continues and R4 applies as for an unanswered question. [paraphrase]
- **R2:** "Stop now" ends the recording and processes it as the menu's "Stop Recording" does for a manual recording, and as a meeting end does for a detected one (watching continues). Every saved track (app, microphone, mix, and the record-only output) then ends at the same point on the recording's timeline: 10 seconds after the last moment speech was heard. Errors: when no speech was heard at all in the recording, when that point is not earlier than the stop (speech came back moments before "Stop now"), or when the cut fails, the recording is processed uncut and the record-only sidecar's stop time is the real stop (a failed cut is logged and leaves every track as recorded); the recording is never discarded. [paraphrase]
- **R3:** When speech is heard while a question is open, the question is withdrawn from Notification Center, and the next one comes only after another full configured time without speech. Errors: an answer to a withdrawn question changes nothing. [inferred]
- **R4:** "Keep recording" withdraws the question and the recording continues uncut; the next question comes only after another full configured time without speech, counted from the answer. A question left unanswered stays until it is replaced by a fresh one, stating the longer silence, after another full configured time, or until R3 or R7 withdraws it. Errors: an answer arriving after the recording ended changes nothing. [paraphrase]
- **R5:** After "Stop now" ended a detected meeting's recording, that app's meeting is not detected, recorded or asked about again until the app's call signal has disappeared once; other apps are detected as usual, and a silence stop of another app adds its own hold without releasing the first. Errors: none beyond the detector's own. [inferred]
- **R6:** Settings → General has a "Long Silence" section with a switch "Ask to stop after a long silence" (default on) and a minutes value (1–60, default 5) that is disabled while the switch is off. Both are stored and survive a restart; a change applies to a running recording at its next poll, and switching off withdraws an open question. Errors: values outside 1–60 are clamped. [paraphrase]
- **R7:** Only one question is open at a time: while gh-54's "meeting seems to have ended" question is open, no silence question is posted and an open one is withdrawn in the same poll; when that question closes without ending the recording, the silence count restarts at that moment. A recording that ends any other way (duration cap, monitored app quitting, "Stop Watching", the menu's "Stop Recording", gh-54's countdown) ends exactly as without this spec and withdraws an open silence question. Errors: none beyond R2. [inferred]
- **R8:** Every silence question posted, withdrawn (with its reason) or answered with "Stop now", and every re-detection hold set and released, writes one line to the diagnostic log the app keeps on disk, with the silence length in whole seconds where it applies. No line carries a meeting title, participant name or transcript content. Errors: none beyond the log write itself. [inferred]

## Boundaries

- Speech is judged by level only, with the existing thresholds; a voice-activity model (FluidVAD) for the judgment is a follow-up, if real use shows typing or room noise keeps resetting the count or quiet speech is missed. [inferred]
- Only the trailing silence of a recording stopped through the question is cut; leading silence at the start of a recording is not trimmed (follow-up if wanted). [inferred]
- The existing "Recording Appears Silent" notification, the menu-bar tint and the "Warn after" setting stay as they are. [inferred]
- No menu-bar indication of an open question, no `/state` or automation-API field for it, and no automation route to answer it (follow-up for e2e lanes). [inferred]
- Pause (#46) is not built here. Its spec sets the policy's `held` input while a recording is paused, so paused time never counts as silence and the count restarts at resume, and it owns mapping a wall-clock cut point onto a paused recording's timeline, for gh-54's cut and this one alike. [inferred]
- Depends on gh-54 for the notification category and answer registry, the all-tracks cut, the record-only sidecar stop time, the meeting-end poll loop and the auto-stop log line; this spec adds no second copy of any of them. [inferred]
- The question uses gh-54's time-sensitive urgency; no separate setting for it. [inferred]
