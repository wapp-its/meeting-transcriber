## Goal & Context

After this spec, a finished meeting in All Recordings can be processed again from its own audio, from its row, without finding and importing the file by hand:

- **"Transcribe Again…"** runs the whole processing again with the current settings: transcription, speaker identification, the Name Speakers dialog and the notes. [inferred]
- **"Identify Speakers Again…"** keeps the text and works out again only who said what, with a speaker method and count chosen in its dialog. Then the speakers are named again and the notes are made again. [inferred]

In both cases the meeting stays one row. It gets the new transcript and notes only when the new run is done and its speakers are named. The earlier transcript and notes then stay beside the new ones under names of their own, and the row shows the new result. A re-run that fails or is cancelled leaves the meeting exactly as it was. [paraphrase]

Why it matters: the settings and the app get better after a meeting was processed: another engine or a model trained for the language, a vocabulary with the meeting's names, an overlap-aware speaker method such as Nemotron 3, or a pipeline fix such as #213 (turns in two-track transcripts placed when they are spoken). An import by hand starts a new meeting instead: a second row, with the file name as its title, no meeting start time, and for a recorded call only if the user picks the call-audio and microphone files together. [inferred]

Who it is for: everyone who records meetings with the app. Why now: the owner picked it on 2026-11-12, after the All Recordings window (gh-170) and the per-meeting file record (gh-97) were built. [paraphrase]

Upstream check (2026-11-12): the original project has no issue or pull request about transcribing a finished meeting again or identifying its speakers again. Related only: its open PR 971 makes meeting notes from saved transcripts (notes only, no new transcription); its open PR 960 deletes recordings after a retention period (the overlap with gh-97 is tracked in #188); its merged PR 924 turns the one-day wait before unnamed meetings get automatic names into a setting, which reaches the fork with the next upstream merge.

## Architecture & Data Models

- **A re-run is the same meeting.** It keeps the job's id, so the row, the meeting's file record (gh-97), its finished-job entry and its Notion page stay one meeting. Like Retry (`PipelineQueue+Retry.swift`) it puts the job back into the pipeline's queue, but from the finished state. A finished job has usually left the queue already (60 s after it finished), so the job is rebuilt from its finished-job entry (`TerminalJobRecord`: title, app, start time, participants) and from its record (the source audio and the run inputs below). [inferred]
- **Run inputs in the record.** From this version on, the meeting's record (`MeetingFileRecord`) also keeps what a re-run needs and the finished-job entry lacks: the mic delay, the recording's pauses, the protocol template and background the meeting was made with, and the Notion database and page it was exported to. Each is optional when decoded, like gh-97's later fields. A record without them (a meeting from before this version, or a record rewritten by an older build, which drops fields it does not know) re-runs with no mic delay, no pause markers, the template the rules pick for its title, no background, and the page id from the finished-job entry. [inferred]
- **The source audio** is what the record lists as the meeting's source tracks: the mix and the call-audio and microphone tracks of a recording, or the user's own file for an import. A re-run reads them where they are and never moves, copies or changes them. The 16 kHz copies are made again and removed after naming, as for a new meeting. [inferred]
- **The meeting's current files stay until the re-run succeeds.** The re-run writes its transcript draft, transcript, notes, segments and naming files under names of its own, never under the meeting's current names. At success (speakers confirmed or skipped, notes saved or not wanted) the current transcript and notes are renamed in their folder to earlier versions, and the new files take the current names and folder. The record follows each step. On failure, cancel or an unanswered naming, the re-run's own files are removed and the meeting is as it was. [paraphrase]
- **Earlier versions.** The name is the current file name with `.until-<yyyyMMdd_HHmmss>` before the extension, stamped with the re-run's date and time in local time: `<name>.until-20261113_091502.txt` and `.md`. In Finder and in a plain name sort it comes right after the current files (the `u` sorts after the `m` of `.md` and the `t` of `.txt`), and several earlier versions sort by date. A name that is taken gets `-2`, `-3`, … after the stamp; nothing is ever overwritten. The name never ends in `)` and holds no ` (`, so gh-190's rule never reads it as an additional report, like Regenerate's `.previous-<stamp>` copies. The record lists both earlier files as owned files, appended (`appendKept`) under the existing role `previousProtocol`, which gh-190 already uses for kept copies and reports; a new role would make a build without this spec set the record file aside. So Move Meeting to Trash moves them with the meeting's other files, and its confirmation counts them. The automatic deletion (gh-97) deletes only audio, so it leaves them, as it leaves the transcript and the notes. [inferred]
- **Stages.** A re-run runs the stages of a new meeting: transcription, speaker identification, the Name Speakers dialog with its own Re-run, the notes, the Notion export. It does not repeat the audio move into the output folder, the processed-recordings ledger or the rename to the AI title. [inferred]
- **Identify Speakers Again without transcription.** When the meeting's word timings are kept (next bullet), the run makes the 16 kHz copies from the source audio, diarizes them with the method and count from its dialog, and re-labels the kept segments. This is the path the naming dialog's late Re-run takes today (`SpeakerNamingSession+Late.swift`), started from a finished meeting. The words stay as they are; only the speakers change. Without kept timings, the run transcribes first, with the current settings. [inferred]
- **Word timings kept.** Today the transcript segments file (`<basename>_segments.json`: start, end and text per segment) is deleted with the naming files once the speakers are named (`SpeakerNamingStore.cleanupSidecarFiles`). From this version on it stays for a finished meeting whose raw transcript is kept separately. It is written readable only by the user (gh-97 R13) and listed in the record under the existing role `segments`, so Move Meeting to Trash takes it along. #214 proposes a segment file next to the transcript for other tools; if that lands first, Identify Speakers Again may read its timings from that file instead (A4). [inferred]
- **Row decision.** The two items come from the pure row decision (`RecordingRowActions`): the job state, the record, and the row's last look at its source audio (`RowAvailability`; for an import, the user's file the record lists), never a file check while a menu is built. The action checks the audio again on the click, as Retry does. [inferred]
- **Two sheets over All Recordings**, like the regenerate sheet. Their content is below; their layout is the accepted mockup (Mockup gate in Decision Context). [paraphrase]
  - **Transcribe Again:** the meeting's name; what the run will use from the current Settings (engine and model, language, vocabulary on or off, speaker method); what happens to the current files (they stay as they are until the new transcript and notes are saved, then stay beside them as earlier versions named with the re-run's date and time; the row shows the new result; additional reports stay; a Notion page is updated, not duplicated); Cancel and "Transcribe Again". [inferred]
  - **Identify Speakers Again:** the meeting's name; the speaker method list and, for Standard, the speaker count, with gh-163's names, descriptions and two-track wording, starting at the Settings' method and expected speakers (2 when Settings says Auto); what happens (the text stays, the speakers are named again, the notes are made again, and the current transcript and notes stay beside the new ones as earlier versions named with the re-run's date and time); for a meeting without kept word timings, a line that the audio is transcribed again first with the current transcription settings; Cancel and "Identify Speakers". [inferred]

## API Contracts

- **No new endpoint.** During a re-run, `GET /v1/jobs/<id>` answers from the live job (waiting, transcribing, …), as for any job in the queue. Afterwards it answers from the finished entry: with the new paths after a success, with the earlier ones after a failed or cancelled re-run. So a job that was done can be live again; `docs/automation-api.md` says so. [inferred]
- `/ui/tree` and `/ui/press` keep their window allowlist; All Recordings stays off it, since it shows meeting titles. [inferred]
- **Record file:** new optional fields only, and no new `MeetingFile.Role`. A build without this spec decodes roles strictly and sets a file it cannot read aside as corrupt (gh-190), and the release and development builds share the record file. [inferred]

## Edge Cases & Constraints

- **Audio gone at the click** (moved to the Trash, deleted automatically, removed in Finder, an imported file moved by the user): nothing is queued and the sheet says the audio is no longer there. A drive that is not connected: nothing is queued and the sheet says so. [inferred]
- **Part of a recording's audio missing:** without the mix, the call-audio and microphone tracks are mixed on the fly, as a paired import is. Without one of the two tracks, the run degrades to the remaining audio as the first run does for a silent track. [inferred]
- **The current files' folder is not reachable** when the re-run would swap in its files: the re-run fails (R6) and the meeting stays as it was. [inferred]
- **Unanswered naming:** the stale-naming cleanup gives a waiting meeting the automatic names after one day (a setting once the original's PR 924 is merged in). For a re-run, it ends the re-run instead, and the meeting stays as it was (A5). [inferred]
- **Quit or crash during a re-run:** at the next launch the re-run resumes like any interrupted job. The current files stay until it succeeds. [inferred]
- **Two builds share the data folder:** while one build re-runs a meeting, the record's outcome is "in progress", so the other build's actions on that meeting stay off, as gh-97's actions already require a finished meeting. [inferred]
- **Automatic deletion (gh-97):** it skips a meeting while it re-runs, since the meeting is not finished then. The recording date does not change. With "Right after the notes", the audio goes right after the re-run's notes are saved, as after the first run. [inferred]
- **Regenerate from File… with an earlier version chosen:** gh-190's rule reads an earlier version with its own `.txt` beside it as a meeting of its own, so new notes made from it never touch the meeting's current notes. [inferred]
- **Notes generation off, or the protocol provider None:** the re-run replaces only the transcript (the earlier one stays as an earlier version); the current notes stay current, and the sheet says so (A12). **Record-only mode:** both items are disabled, since the app does not transcribe in that mode. [inferred]
- **Notion:** with the export on, the re-run's notes go to the meeting's existing page, which is updated, never duplicated (A6). With the export off, the page stays as it is. [inferred]
- **Queue order:** a re-run joins the end of the queue like an import, so a meeting recorded meanwhile waits behind it (A14). [inferred]
- **Last Recording (gh-208):** after a re-run finishes, the meeting is the newest finished one, so the menu's Last Recording section shows it. Its place in All Recordings does not move, since the list is ordered by when the job was first added. [inferred]
- **Voice profiles:** names confirmed in a re-run update the voice profiles like any naming (A9). [inferred]
- **Privacy:** meeting titles and template names appear in log lines only as private values. Tests and probes use the repo's fixture audio, never real meetings. [inferred]
- **Wording:** on screen the app says "notes" (gh-97 A11); "Regenerate Notes…", "Open Notes". [inferred]

## Acceptance Criteria

R1 to R6 are the first slice: built and merged first, they let a user transcribe a finished meeting again end to end. A re-run reaches the Name Speakers dialog, whose own Re-run already lets the user pick another speaker method. R7 to R10 add Identify Speakers Again, which keeps the text. R11 applies to both slices.

- **R1 (slice 1):** Each meeting that enters processing from this version on keeps in its record its mic delay, its pauses, its protocol template and background, and after an export its Notion database and page. Errors: a record without these fields still reads, and its re-run uses the fallbacks in Architecture; a failed record write is logged and does not fail the job, as in gh-97. [inferred]
- **R2 (slice 1):** A finished meeting's row offers "Transcribe Again…" in its "…" menu and its right-click menu, directly after "Regenerate Notes…". Errors: absent for a row without a record, a failed meeting (it keeps Retry), a meeting waiting for speaker names (its dialog has Re-run), and when the row's last look found none of its source audio (moved to the Trash, deleted automatically, not found); disabled while the meeting waits, runs or has a Notion export running, while it has a deletion pending or incomplete, while any regeneration runs (gh-97 A19's rule), while its source audio is only on a drive that is not connected, and in record-only mode. [paraphrase]
- **R3 (slice 1):** Choosing it opens the Transcribe Again sheet of the accepted mockup over All Recordings, with the content in Architecture. Errors: Cancel changes nothing; with notes generation off the sheet says the current notes stay; when the audio is gone or out of reach at the click on "Transcribe Again", nothing is queued and the sheet says which. [inferred]
- **R4 (slice 1):** Confirmed, the meeting's row goes back into processing in place (same row, same place in the list, the status lines of any job), and the meeting appears under "In progress" in the menu. The run keeps the meeting's title, app, start time, participants, mic delay, pauses and track layout (a recorded call's call audio and microphone transcribed and diarized separately), uses the template and background the meeting was made with, and reaches the Name Speakers dialog like a new meeting, unless speaker naming is off. Errors: a second re-run of the same meeting cannot start while one is queued or running; a failure in any stage ends as R6. [paraphrase]
- **R5 (slice 1):** When the speakers are confirmed or skipped and the notes are saved, the meeting's current transcript and notes are renamed in their folder to `<name>.until-<yyyyMMdd_HHmmss>.<ext>` (the re-run's date and time), and the new ones take the current file names and folder. The row, Open Notes, Open Transcript, the Last Recording section and the automation API's readback then show the new result; the record lists the new files and both earlier versions; Move Meeting to Trash moves the earlier versions with the meeting and its confirmation counts them; additional reports are unchanged; with the Notion export on, the meeting's page gets the new notes. Errors: a taken name gets `-2`, `-3`, … after the stamp, and no file is ever overwritten; when a rename or move fails, the steps already done are undone and the re-run ends as R6; a quit in the middle of these renames is completed or undone at the next launch, so the meeting always has a current transcript under its current name; a meeting renamed to its AI title keeps that name, and its earlier versions carry it too. [paraphrase]
- **R6 (slice 1):** A re-run that fails, is cancelled (the row's Cancel, or Dismiss in the menu's "Needs you" group), or stays unnamed past the naming wait leaves the meeting as it was: its transcript, notes, additional reports and kept word timings byte for byte, its row finished with its usual main button, its record outcome and finished-job entry as before, and no file of the re-run left behind. After a failure or an unanswered naming, the row shows one warning line ("Transcribe Again failed: <reason>" or "Transcribe Again ended: the speakers were not named in time"). Errors: a quit or crash during the re-run resumes it at the next launch, and the current files stay until it succeeds; a re-run never moves, changes or deletes the meeting's audio. [inferred]
- **R7 (slice 2):** From this version on, a finished meeting whose raw transcript is kept separately keeps its transcript segments file, readable only by the user and listed in its record; Move Meeting to Trash moves it with the meeting's other files. Errors: with "Save raw transcript separately" off, it is deleted when the speakers are named, as today; Move Audio to Trash and the automatic deletion leave it. [inferred]
- **R8 (slice 2):** A finished meeting's row offers "Identify Speakers Again…" directly after "Transcribe Again…", in both menus. Errors: absent and disabled by R2's rules. [paraphrase]
- **R9 (slice 2):** Choosing it opens the Identify Speakers Again sheet of the accepted mockup, with the content in Architecture. Errors: Cancel changes nothing; for a meeting without kept word timings the sheet says the audio is transcribed again first with the current transcription settings; audio gone or out of reach at the click is handled as in R3. [inferred]
- **R10 (slice 2):** Confirmed for a meeting with kept word timings, no transcription runs: the meeting's audio is diarized with the chosen method and count, the transcript keeps every word and its order and changes only the speaker labels and how lines group into turns, and the Name Speakers dialog opens with the new speakers. Without kept timings, the run is R4's, with the chosen method and count. Success and failure follow R5 and R6, with "Identify Speakers Again" in the warning lines. Errors: speaker identification not available ends as R6 with the naming dialog's sentence for it; a diarization that finds no voice ends as R6 with "found no voices to name"; the naming dialog's own Re-run works as today and never touches the meeting's current files. [inferred]
- **R11 (both slices):** The row decision of R2 and R8, the run inputs' fallbacks and the sheets' texts are pure functions with unit tests for every job state, record outcome, origin, audio presence, record-only mode and a running regeneration. The re-run is tested in the pipeline's test setup with stub engines and a temporary folder: success keeps the earlier files byte for byte under their earlier-version names and puts the new files under the current names, also when an earlier-version name is taken; failure, cancel, an unanswered naming and a quit each leave the current files byte for byte; the record follows; a Notion page is reused through a stub exporter; Identify Speakers Again leaves every word unchanged. Each new control has one ViewInspector wiring test located by an `A11yID` constant. Both build variants run the suite. `docs/architecture/pipeline.md` and `docs/architecture/diarization.md` describe the re-run and the kept timings, `docs/automation-api.md` the readback during a re-run. Errors: no error surface beyond R1 to R10. [inferred]
