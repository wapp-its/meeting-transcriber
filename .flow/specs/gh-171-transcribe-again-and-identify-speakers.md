## Goal & Context

After this spec, a finished meeting in All Recordings can be processed again from its own audio, from its row, without finding and importing the file by hand:

- **"Transcribe Again…"** runs the whole processing again with the current settings: transcription, speaker identification, the Name Speakers dialog and the notes. [inferred]
- **"Identify Speakers Again…"** keeps the text and works out again only who said what, with a speaker method and count chosen in its dialog. Then the speakers are named again and the notes are made again. [inferred]

In both cases the meeting stays one row. It gets the new transcript and notes only when the new run is done and its speakers are named. The earlier transcript and notes then stay beside the new ones under names of their own, and the row shows the new result. A re-run that fails or is cancelled leaves the meeting exactly as it was. [paraphrase]

Why it matters: the settings and the app get better after a meeting was processed: another engine or a model trained for the language, a vocabulary with the meeting's names, an overlap-aware speaker method such as Nemotron 3, or a pipeline fix such as #213 (turns in two-track transcripts placed when they are spoken). An import by hand starts a new meeting instead: a second row, with the file name as its title, no meeting start time, and for a recorded call only if the user picks the call-audio and microphone files together. [inferred]

Who it is for: everyone who records meetings with the app. Why now: the owner picked it on 2026-11-12, after the All Recordings window (gh-170) and the per-meeting file record (gh-97) were built. [paraphrase]

Upstream check (2026-11-12, again 2026-11-14): the original project has no issue or pull request about transcribing a finished meeting again or identifying its speakers again. Related only: its open PR 971 makes meeting notes from saved transcripts (notes only, no new transcription); its open PR 970 retries meeting notes that failed; its PR 960, merged on 2026-11-12 and in the fork since, deletes recordings after a retention period, which the fork gates with the meeting's record (`docs/architecture/pipeline.md`, recording retention); its PR 924, merged and in the fork since, made the wait before unnamed meetings get automatic names a setting (`AppSettings.staleNamingTimeout`).

## Architecture & Data Models

- **A re-run is the same meeting.** It keeps the job's id, so the row, the meeting's file record (gh-97), its finished-job entry and its Notion page stay one meeting. Like Retry (`PipelineQueue+Retry.swift`) it puts the job back into the pipeline's queue, but from the finished state. A finished job has usually left the queue already (60 s after it finished), so the job is rebuilt from its finished-job entry (`TerminalJobRecord`: title, app, start time, participants) and from its record (the source audio and the run inputs below). If the finished job is still in the queue (in its 60 s, or held there after completion, `PipelineJob.isHeldAfterCompletion`: by a failed Notion export that waits for "Retry Notion Export", or by meeting notes that failed and wait for "Retry Meeting Notes"), the re-run takes over that entry: its removal is cancelled, the marker keeps the entry as it was (its Notion export state, and its notes failure with the names kept for the retry), and every end but success puts the entry back as it was, so a failed export or failed notes keep their retry. The rebuilt job carries none of the entry's notes-retry fields (`notesFailure`, `notesSpeakerNames`, `generatesNotesOnly`, `notesBasename`), so it never runs as a notes-only job. A job of the meeting that waits, runs, waits for names or exports is refused, a notes retry included. [inferred]
- **Run inputs in the record.** From this version on, the meeting's record (`MeetingFileRecord`) also keeps what a re-run needs and the finished-job entry lacks: the mic delay, the recording's pauses, the mic's muted stretches (A20; in the fork since the 2026-11-15 upstream merge), the protocol template and background the meeting was made with, and the Notion database and page it was exported to. Today these live only on the queued job (`PipelineJob.micDelay`, `pauses`, `micMuteIntervals`, `protocolTemplate`, `protocolBackground`, `notionDatabaseID`, `notionPageID`) and its snapshot entry, which are gone once the job leaves the queue. Each is optional when decoded, like gh-97's later fields. A record without them (a meeting from before this version, or a record rewritten by an older build, which drops fields it does not know) re-runs with no mic delay, no pause markers, no mute markers, the template the rules pick for its title, no background, and the page id from the finished-job entry. [inferred]
- **Run settings.** A re-run runs with the settings its sheet showed (A3). The engine instances follow Settings' model, language and vocabulary live (`EngineController`), but the queue captures the engine choice, speaker identification on or off, the expected speakers and VAD when it is built (`PipelineController.makeQueue`) and renews them only when every job it holds is finished (`PipelineController.rebuild`). So admission reads these four from Settings into the re-run job, the stages use the job's values over the queue's, and the engine instance for the job's choice comes from the engine controller. Identify Speakers Again's method and count from its sheet travel the same way. [inferred]
- **The source audio** is what the record lists as the meeting's source tracks: the mix and the call-audio and microphone tracks of a recording (roles `mix`, `appTrack`, `micTrack`), or the user's own file for an import. A re-run reads them where they are and never moves, copies or changes them. The 16 kHz copies are made again and removed after naming, as for a new meeting. [inferred]
- **The meeting's current files stay until the re-run succeeds.** The re-run writes its transcript draft, transcript, notes, segments and naming files under names of its own, never under the meeting's current names. At success (speakers confirmed or skipped, notes saved or not wanted) the current transcript and notes are renamed in their folder to earlier versions, and the new files take the current names and folder. The record follows each step. On failure, cancel or an unanswered naming, the re-run's own files are removed and the meeting is as it was. [paraphrase]
- **Re-run marker.** From admission until it ends, the job carries a re-run marker, and the meeting's record an optional field with the same marker: the re-run's stamp, the record's outcome before the re-run, the build and process that admitted it (the bundle identifier, and the process id with its start time as `DeletionProcess` keeps them), the re-run's own files with their file identity, and, once the swap starts, its list of renames. The record's outcome reads "in progress" meanwhile, as for any job that enters processing, so the other build's actions and the recording retention leave the meeting alone. Admission writes the record's marker under the record file's lock, before the job enters the queue, and refuses a record that is not finished or already carries one, so a second re-run cannot start, also not from the other build. A build acts only on markers its own bundle identifier wrote. [inferred]
- **A re-run job is saved in a file of its own** beside the queue's snapshot, named with the build's bundle identifier, and never in the shared `pipeline_queue.json` (A24): both builds read that file at launch, and a build without this spec would restore a re-run job as a new meeting and write over the meeting's current files. Loading merges the two files; a live re-run job wins over a finished entry of the same id. [inferred]
- **How a re-run ends.** The record is the re-run's journal. Every end but success (a failure, Cancel, Dismiss, an unanswered naming) first marks the record's marker as ending, then removes the re-run's own files, then in one record update restores the outcome from the marker, clears the marker and notes the stamp as ended, and only then takes the job out of the queue and its file. A file that fails the check below or cannot be removed stays where it is, and so does the marker, listing only the files left, while the outcome is restored: the next launch and the meeting's next Transcribe Again or Identify Speakers Again try those files again first, and while any is left the sheet refuses with a red line (A27). Success commits the same way (Swap order). The note is one optional record field, the stamp with committed or ended, kept until the next re-run is admitted. A record write that fails leaves the job in its file, and the next launch acts on what the record says. [inferred]
- **At launch** this build reads its re-run file before it ends any marker, which `reconcileInterruptedDeletions` would otherwise do before any queue exists, and decides by the record. The rows that resume or finish a job run when the queue restores the re-run file; the others run beside `reconcileInterruptedDeletions`. `restorePendingNotionExports` (`PipelineController+Notion.swift`) builds the first queue at launch only when every job in `pipeline_queue.json` is finished and one is held after completion; a live re-run job in this build's re-run file counts there as a job that is not finished, so the re-run waits for the first queue as any saved job does. A re-run job from the re-run file never goes through `loadSnapshot`'s `discardFinishedJobs`, which drops a finished job that is not held and removes its naming data. [inferred]

  | The record says | Job in this build's re-run file | At launch |
  |---|---|---|
  | marker of the stamp, neither ending nor with a list of renames | yes | the re-run resumes |
  | marker of the stamp with a list of renames | yes or no | the swap is completed when every re-run file it names is there, else undone from the list; then as committed or ended |
  | marker of the stamp marked as ending | yes or no | the files left are removed and the re-run ends as above |
  | the stamp noted as committed | yes | the finished-job entry is written, the job turns into an ordinary finished job, and with the export on its Notion export starts once from the saved notes, to the page's database (A26); the launch's handling of interrupted exports only marks them failed, so it does not cover this |
  | the stamp noted as ended, or no marker and no note (a record an older build rewrote) | yes | the re-run ends as R6 with the outcome from the job's marker |
  | a marker of this build, not ending | no, and its process has ended | the re-run ends as R6 |
  | anything | the file cannot be read | nothing changes; every marker stays |

- **Only the re-run's own files.** The end paths, the swap's undo and the launch recovery remove or rename a file only when its path still leads to the regular file the marker names, by file identity and in the resolved folder it names (the check `MeetingFileRemover` makes before a deletion); the swap checks the meeting's current files the same way against the record. A file that fails the check stays, the marker stays, and the reason is logged as a private value. [inferred]
- **The finished-job entry stays as it was during a re-run.** Unlike Retry, a re-run does not remove it. While the job is live, the row and `GET /v1/jobs/<id>` answer from the live job (a job in both is listed from the live job); the rebuilt job keeps the entry's time of first adding, so the row keeps its place. Only success rewrites the entry; every other end leaves it byte for byte. [inferred]
- **Swap order.** At success the swap first writes its list of renames into the record's marker, then renames the current transcript and notes to their earlier-version names, then the re-run's files to the current names. One record update then lists the new files as present, with the identity they have now, appends the earlier versions, clears the marker, notes the stamp as committed and sets the outcome to finished: that update is the commit point. Then the finished-job entry is written; once that write reports it landed (`TerminalJobStore.record` gets a result its other callers may ignore), the job turns into an ordinary finished job, leaves the re-run file, and its Notion export runs as for a new meeting; that export and its retries go to the page's database (A26). A failed export is shown as an export failure, as today, and does not undo the swap. The swap waits while a regeneration runs, as Move Meeting to Trash does. The re-run's notes are saved under its own stem by `saveGeneratedNotes` (`PipelineQueue+Stages.swift`), which also queues the pending export; that export starts only after the commit. Every rename into a current name refuses a name that is taken (a move that never replaces a file): notes that appeared next to the current transcript meanwhile (Generate Meeting Notes from Transcript writes `<stem>.md` beside a transcript without notes) are never overwritten, and the re-run ends as R6 instead. A meeting without current notes (they failed, or no provider made any) gets no earlier notes; the new notes take the name the meeting's notes have by its current transcript's stem. After the commit the finished job's transcript, notes and stem are the current names, which a later retry and the held-job cleanup read. [inferred]
- **Earlier versions.** The name is the current file name with `.until-<yyyyMMdd_HHmmss>` before the extension, stamped with the re-run's date and time in local time: `<name>.until-20261113_091502.txt` and `.md`. In Finder and in a plain name sort it comes right after the current files (the `u` sorts after the `m` of `.md` and the `t` of `.txt`), and several earlier versions sort by date. A name that is taken gets `-2`, `-3`, … after the stamp; nothing is ever overwritten. The name never ends in `)` and holds no ` (`, so gh-190's rule never reads it as an additional report, like Regenerate's `.previous-<stamp>` copies. The record lists both earlier files as owned files, appended (`appendKept`) under the existing role `previousProtocol`, which gh-190 already uses for kept copies and reports; a new role would make a build without this spec set the record file aside. So Move Meeting to Trash moves them with the meeting's other files, and its confirmation counts them. The recording retention deletes only audio, so it leaves them, as it leaves the transcript and the notes. [inferred]
- **Stages.** A re-run runs the stages of a new meeting: transcription, speaker identification, the Name Speakers dialog with its own Re-run, the notes, the Notion export. It does not repeat the audio move into the output folder, the processed-recordings ledger or the rename to the AI title. [inferred]
- **A failure a new meeting only warns about ends a re-run** (A25). A new meeting finishes with a warning when speaker identification fails or is not available (`diarize` catches the one and skips on the other) and when its notes cannot be made (`protocolText` returns nil) or saved (`saveProtocolLocally` returns false). In a re-run each of these ends the re-run as R6 before the job's usual completion runs, whether the naming was confirmed or skipped. With speaker identification off in Settings, none runs, so none fails. Since the 2026-11-15 upstream merge, `protocolText` also records the failure on the job (`recordNotesFailure`, and `generateProtocol` the names in `notesSpeakerNames`), which would hold a new meeting for a notes retry; a re-run that ends is never held that way, and it posts none of the done notifications (`PipelineQueue.doneNotification`), whose texts say a transcript or notes were saved. [inferred]
- **Identify Speakers Again without transcription.** When the meeting's word timings are kept (next bullet), the run makes the 16 kHz copies from the source audio, diarizes them with the method and count from its dialog, and re-labels the kept segments, with the job's mute spans as annotations (`micMuteSpansOnTranscript`) as the late re-run passes them. This is the path the naming dialog's late Re-run takes today (`SpeakerNamingSession+Late.swift`), started from a finished meeting. The words stay as they are; only the speakers change. Without kept timings, the run transcribes first, with the current settings. [inferred]
- **Word timings kept.** Today the transcript segments file (`<basename>_segments.json`: start, end and text per segment) is deleted with the naming files once the speakers are named (`SpeakerNamingStore.cleanupSidecarFiles`, whose `sidecarSuffixes` include `_segments.json`), and on its own when the raw transcript is not kept (`SpeakerNamingSession.removeTranscriptSegments`); since the 2026-11-15 upstream merge also when a job held after completion finishes (`applyJobState`, its `isHeldAfterCompletion` branch). From this version on it stays for a finished meeting whose raw transcript is kept separately. It is written readable only by the user (gh-97 R13) and listed in the record under the existing role `segments`, so Move Meeting to Trash takes it along. The record keeps with it the label its microphone segments carry (the queue's `micLabel`, Settings' mic name at that run), since re-labelling splits a two-track meeting's segments by that label (`DiarizationProcess.labelSegments`) and the mic name can change before Identify Speakers Again; without the label, every segment not labelled as the call audio's counts as the microphone's. A re-run writes its own segments file under a name of its own; at success it takes the current name and the earlier segments file is deleted, since only the current transcript is offered for Identify Speakers Again (A22); before success the earlier one stays as it is. #214 proposes a segment file next to the transcript for other tools; if that lands first, Identify Speakers Again may read its timings from that file instead (A4). [inferred]
- **Row decision.** The two items come from the pure row decision (`RecordingRowActions`): the job state, the record, and the row's last look at its source audio (`RowAvailability`; for an import, the user's file the record lists), never a file check while a menu is built. The action checks the audio again on the click, as Retry does. [inferred]

## API Contracts

- **No new endpoint.** During a re-run, `GET /v1/jobs/<id>` answers from the live job (waiting, transcribing, …), as for any job in the queue. Afterwards it answers from the finished entry: with the new paths after a success, with the earlier ones after a failed or cancelled re-run. So a job that was done can be live again; `docs/automation-api.md` says so. [inferred]
- `/ui/tree` and `/ui/press` keep their window allowlist; All Recordings stays off it, since it shows meeting titles. [inferred]
- **Record file:** new optional fields only, and no new `MeetingFile.Role`. A build without this spec decodes roles strictly and sets a file it cannot read aside as corrupt (gh-190), and the release and development builds share the record file. [inferred]

## Edge Cases & Constraints

- **Audio gone at the click** (moved to the Trash, deleted automatically, removed in Finder, an imported file moved by the user): nothing is queued and the sheet says the audio is no longer there. A drive that is not connected: nothing is queued and the sheet says so. [inferred]
- **Part of a recording's audio missing:** without the mix, the call-audio and microphone tracks are mixed on the fly, as a paired import is. Without one of the two tracks, the run degrades to the remaining audio as the first run does for a silent track. [inferred]
- **The current files' folder is not reachable** when the re-run would swap in its files: the re-run fails (R6) and the meeting stays as it was. [inferred]
- **Unanswered naming:** at launch, the stale-naming cleanup gives a waiting meeting the automatic names once the wait set in Settings has passed (`AppSettings.staleNamingTimeout`: 1 day, 7 days or never). For a re-run, it ends the re-run instead, and the meeting stays as it was (A5). With never, a re-run waits until its speakers are named or skipped or it is cancelled, as a new meeting does. [inferred]
- **Quit or crash during a re-run:** at the next launch this build resumes the re-run from its own re-run file, by the table under Architecture. The current files stay until it succeeds. [inferred]
- **Two builds share the data folder:** while one build re-runs a meeting, the record's outcome is "in progress", so the other build's actions on that meeting stay off, as gh-97's actions already require a finished meeting. The other build never restores the re-run job (A24) and leaves its marker alone. [inferred]
- **Recording retention** (the original's, with the fork's record gate; `docs/architecture/pipeline.md`): it skips a meeting while it re-runs, since its record is not finished then (`MeetingFileRecord.allowsRetention`) and an unfinished job holds its tracks (`RecordingRetentionPolicy.decide`). A re-run does not move the audio, so it writes no new `RecordingRetentionRegistry` entry, and the period still counts from the recording's start. With "right after the notes" (0 days), the audio goes right after the re-run ends, as after the first run. [inferred]
- **A meeting whose notes failed** (held for "Retry Meeting Notes", since the 2026-11-15 upstream merge): it is a finished meeting, so both items are offered; the re-run takes over its held entry (Architecture), a success with new notes ends the retry offer, a success without them (notes off, A12) keeps it, now for the new transcript, and every other end brings the entry back with its retry. [inferred]
- **Generate Meeting Notes from Transcript** (the menu bar's item and `POST /v1/jobs/notes/generate`): its notes-only jobs' records list no source audio, so neither item is offered for them (R2). Picked for a meeting's current transcript while that meeting re-runs, it may write notes under the meeting's current notes name; the swap then refuses that taken name and the re-run ends as R6. [inferred]
- **Regenerate from File… with an earlier version chosen:** gh-190's rule reads an earlier version with its own `.txt` beside it as a meeting of its own, so new notes made from it never touch the meeting's current notes. [inferred]
- **Notes generation off, or the protocol provider None:** the re-run replaces only the transcript (the earlier one stays as an earlier version); the current notes stay current, and the sheet says so (A12). **Record-only mode:** both items are disabled, since the app does not transcribe in that mode. [inferred]
- **Notion:** with the export on, the re-run's notes go to the meeting's existing page, which is updated, never duplicated (A6), in the database the page was made in, also when Settings now name another (A26). With the export off, the page stays as it is. [inferred]
- **Queue order:** a re-run joins the end of the queue like an import, so a meeting recorded meanwhile waits behind it (A14). [inferred]
- **Last Recording (gh-208):** after a re-run finishes, the meeting is the newest finished one, so the menu's Last Recording section shows it. Its place in All Recordings does not move, since the list is ordered by when the job was first added. [inferred]
- **Voice profiles:** names confirmed in a re-run update the voice profiles like any naming (A9). [inferred]
- **Privacy:** meeting titles, template names, file names and failure reasons appear in log lines only as private values. Tests and probes use the repo's fixture audio, never real meetings. [inferred]
- **Wording:** on screen the app says "notes" (gh-97 A11); "Regenerate Notes…", "Open Notes". [inferred]

## Acceptance Criteria

R1 to R6 are the first slice, built and reviewed first: on their own they let a user transcribe a finished meeting again end to end, and a re-run reaches the Name Speakers dialog, whose own Re-run already lets the user pick another speaker method. R7 to R10 add Identify Speakers Again, which keeps the text; they are built after the first slice, in the same pull request. R11 applies to both slices.

- **R1 (slice 1):** Each meeting that enters processing from this version on keeps in its record its mic delay, its pauses, its protocol template and background, and after an export its Notion database and page. Errors: a record without these fields still reads, and its re-run uses the fallbacks in Architecture; a failed record write is logged and does not fail the job, as in gh-97. [inferred]
- **R2 (slice 1):** A finished meeting's row offers "Transcribe Again…" in its "…" menu and its right-click menu, directly after "Regenerate Notes…", with the separators of D5. Errors: absent for a row without a record, a failed meeting (it keeps Retry), a meeting waiting for speaker names (its dialog has Re-run), and when the row's last look found none of its source audio (moved to the Trash, deleted automatically, not found); disabled while the meeting waits, runs or has a Notion export running, while it has a deletion pending or incomplete, while any regeneration runs (gh-97 A19's rule), while its source audio is only on a drive that is not connected, and in record-only mode.
